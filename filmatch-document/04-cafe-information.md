# About Filmatch

Content version: 1.0 | Effective: 3 November 2026

## Identity and purpose

**Filmatch** is a fictional matcha slowbar café pairing whisked matcha drinks with Filipino desserts. Its tagline is **“Matcha, merienda, and a moment to slow down.”**

The café and its assistant, **Ask Filmatch**, exist only as a SAM10 student-project demonstration. There is no real operating branch, ordering service, or delivery service behind this content.

## Fictional location

Unit 4, Bahay Sari Courtyard, 18 Dahon Lane, Barangay Lunti, Biringan City, Philippines.

The venue, street, barangay, and city above are invented. Do not provide map coordinates, travel directions, nearby real landmarks, or claims that this address exists. No verified phone number, email address, social-media account, or map listing is available.

## Slowbar preparation

Each drink is whisked and assembled individually. Preparation estimates begin after staff accept the order.

| Item / situation | Typical estimate |
| --- | --- |
| One drink | 5–8 minutes |
| Two to four drinks in one order | 10–15 minutes for the complete drink order |
| Ready-to-serve dessert | 2–4 minutes |
| Mixed drink-and-dessert order | Usually follows the drink estimate |
| Busy periods | Allow an additional 5–10 minutes |

Estimates are not guarantees. Queue length, staffing, and stock can change the wait. Ask Filmatch cannot view the queue or track preparation. Orders larger than four drinks require a staff estimate; bulk preorders are not offered in this content version.

## Service style

Filmatch offers dine-in and takeaway. In this fictional operating model, customers order and pay at the counter. Cash and a staff-presented QR payment option are accepted. No real QR code, account number, online checkout, or payment link is part of this project.
