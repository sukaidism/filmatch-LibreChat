# Filmatch Customisation Guide

Content version: 1.0 | Effective: 3 November 2026

These are invented options for the fictional Filmatch menu. Prices are PHP and apply per drink. Ask Filmatch may explain options and calculate a quoted total; it cannot submit or confirm an order.

## Options by drink

| Drink | Sweetness | Ice | Main milk / beverage | Matcha strength |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Classic Matcha Latte | 0%, 25%, 50%, or 100% added simple syrup | Regular, less, or no ice for iced; not applicable for hot | Dairy, oat, soy, or coconut | Light, standard, or strong |
| Coconut Cloud Matcha | Fixed coconut syrup; no added simple syrup | Regular, less, or no ice | Coconut only | Light, standard, or strong |
| Ube Matcha Latte | 0%, 25%, 50%, or 100% added simple syrup; ube base stays sweetened | Regular, less, or no ice | Dairy, oat, soy, or coconut | Light, standard, or strong |
| Strawberry Matcha Latte | 0%, 25%, 50%, or 100% added simple syrup; fruit base stays sweetened | Regular, less, or no ice | Dairy, oat, soy, or coconut | Light, standard, or strong |
| Pure Matcha | Unsweetened by default; 25%, 50%, or 100% simple syrup available free | Regular, less, or no ice for iced; not applicable for hot | None; milk cannot be added | Light, standard, or strong |

No substitutions outside this table are documented.

## Sweetness

- The full added-simple-syrup portion is 20 mL: 100% = 20 mL, 50% = 10 mL, 25% = 5 mL, and 0% = 0 mL.
- Classic, Ube, and Strawberry Matcha Latte default to 50% added simple syrup.
- Pure Matcha defaults to 0% added simple syrup.
- Coconut Cloud Matcha has a fixed 15 mL coconut-syrup portion and no adjustable added simple syrup.
- These percentages refer only to added simple syrup. They are not percentages of total sugar or nutritional measurements.
- Selecting 0% does not remove sugar already present in milk alternatives, ube base, or strawberry base. It does not mean “sugar-free.”
- Sweetness changes have no price addition. Sugar substitutes are unavailable.

## Ice and temperature

- Iced drinks default to regular ice. Less ice and no ice have no price addition.
- The prepared liquid quantity stays the same. Less or no ice leaves more empty space in the cup; it does not include extra milk, water, or matcha.
- No-ice drinks are not guaranteed chilled because matcha is whisked with warm water.
- Only Classic Matcha Latte and Pure Matcha have hot versions. Cup sizes and base prices are listed in `01-menu.md`.
- Blended drinks and extra-large sizes are unavailable.

## Milk changes and additions

For Classic, Ube, and Strawberry Matcha Latte:

| Main beverage | Addition to base price |
| --- | --- |
| Dairy milk, default | PHP 0 |
| Oat beverage | PHP 25 |
| Soy beverage | PHP 20 |
| Coconut beverage | PHP 20 |

Coconut Cloud Matcha includes coconut beverage in its PHP 165 base price, so no substitution addition applies. Its main beverage cannot be changed. Pure Matcha has no milk option.

Changing the main milk does not remove the dairy ingredients in the Ube Matcha Latte's ube base. Check `02-ingredients-and-allergens.md` before making any dietary claim.

## Matcha strength

| Strength | Matcha per drink | Addition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Light | 1 g | PHP 0; no discount |
| Standard, default | 2 g | PHP 0 |
| Strong | 3 g | PHP 30 |

All strengths contain caffeine. Caffeine amounts in milligrams are not documented. Strength changes do not change the cup size. More than 3 g, separate matcha shots, and decaffeinated matcha are unavailable.

## Desserts

- Recipes, sweetness, and portions are fixed.
- The separate grated coconut for Kutsinta Trio may be omitted at no price change. This does not guarantee freedom from coconut cross-contact.
- Puto cheese, cassava topping, and bibingka toppings cannot be removed or substituted.
- No extra sauces, pearls, whipped cream, toppings, or dessert substitutions are offered.

## Quoting a total

Total = base menu price + one applicable milk addition + one applicable strength addition + any separate menu items. Sweetness and ice changes are free. Do not apply unpublished discounts or additional fees.

Examples:

- Classic Matcha Latte with oat beverage: PHP 145 + PHP 25 = **PHP 170**.
- Strong Ube Matcha Latte with soy beverage: PHP 175 + PHP 20 + PHP 30 = **PHP 225**. The ube base still contains milk.
- Classic Matcha Latte with oat beverage plus Puto Duo: PHP 145 + PHP 25 + PHP 65 = **PHP 235**.
- Coconut Cloud Matcha plus Kutsinta Trio: PHP 165 + PHP 65 = **PHP 230**.
