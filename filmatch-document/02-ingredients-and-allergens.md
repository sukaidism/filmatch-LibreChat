# Filmatch Ingredients and Allergen Information

Content version: 1.0 | Effective: 3 November 2026

This is an invented recipe specification for the fictional Filmatch student project. It is not a verified food-safety declaration for a real business. A real café would need supplier-label checks, recipe validation, and staff procedures before using it.

## Drink ingredients

Water and ice use the same fictional filtered-water supply. Matcha powder contains green tea only. Simple syrup contains sugar and water.

### Prepared bases

- **Ube base:** purple yam, condensed milk, evaporated milk, butter, and sugar. It contains milk. Replacing the drink's main milk does not remove milk from this base.
- **Strawberry base:** strawberries, sugar, lemon juice, and water. It has fixed sugar content.
- **Coconut syrup:** coconut extract, sugar, and water. It has fixed sugar content.

## Milk and beverage options

These are the project recipes for the available beverages, rather than verified commercial supplier labels.

| --- | --- | --- |

No almond beverage, lactose-free dairy milk, or other milk alternative is offered. Plant-based beverages are not automatically unsweetened. No certified gluten-free or allergen-free claim is made for any option.

## Shared preparation and limits

- Drinks share preparation surfaces, utensils, and equipment that handle milk and soy. Dessert handling also involves egg, wheat, and coconut.
- Cleaning between orders does not establish that cross-contact has been eliminated.
- No item is guaranteed safe for an allergy, coeliac disease, or another medical dietary requirement.
- A milk substitution changes only the main beverage component. It does not alter prepared bases, toppings, desserts, or shared-equipment conditions.
- Removing the coconut garnish from kutsinta does not justify a coconut-free guarantee.
- Ingredient quantities, calories, caffeine in milligrams, nutritional values, supplier certifications, and laboratory testing are not documented.
- All matcha drinks contain caffeine. Reduced matcha still contains caffeine; a decaffeinated option is unavailable.

## How Ask Filmatch should answer

Name the documented ingredients and limitations directly. For an allergy question, recommend checking with café staff before ordering. Do not label an item “safe,” “allergen-free,” or medically suitable. Do not make up supplier assurances, nutritional values, or allergy substitutions.

Example: “Our ube base contains condensed milk, evaporated milk, and butter. Switching to oat beverage does not make the Ube Matcha Latte dairy-free. We also use shared equipment; please check with staff before ordering if you have an allergy.”
