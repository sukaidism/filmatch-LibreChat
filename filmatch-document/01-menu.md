# Filmatch Menu

Content version: 1.0 | Effective: 3 November 2026

Filmatch is a fictional matcha slowbar café created for the SAM10 student project. All products, prices, availability, and business details below are invented. Prices are in Philippine pesos (PHP). This menu describes the project's standard offering, not live inventory.

## Matcha drinks

All drinks use unsweetened matcha powder. A standard serving contains 2 g of matcha. Milk-based drinks use dairy milk unless changed. Listed prices include the standard recipe; paid customisations are additional. Every matcha drink contains caffeine.

| ID | Drink | Description | Serving | Base price | Standard availability |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D01 | Classic Matcha Latte | Whisked matcha with dairy milk; smooth, grassy, and lightly sweetened. | Iced 16 oz or hot 12 oz | PHP 145 | Every opening day |
| D02 | Coconut Cloud Matcha | Matcha with a coconut beverage and coconut syrup; light and gently sweet. Dairy-free by recipe, with shared-equipment cross-contact possible. | Iced 16 oz | PHP 165 | Every opening day |
| D03 | Ube Matcha Latte | Matcha and dairy milk layered over a sweet ube base made with dairy ingredients. | Iced 16 oz | PHP 175 | Every opening day |
| D04 | Strawberry Matcha Latte | Matcha and dairy milk layered over a sweet strawberry fruit base. | Iced 16 oz | PHP 175 | Every opening day |
| D05 | Pure Matcha | Matcha whisked with water, with no milk or sweetener by default; the most direct matcha flavour. | Iced 12 oz or hot 8 oz | PHP 120 | Every opening day |

Cup sizes are nominal capacity, including ice and any foam. Hot and iced Classic Matcha Latte have the same price. Hot and iced Pure Matcha have the same price. No other sizes or hot versions are offered in this content version.

## Availability and pricing rules

- The café is closed on Mondays. “Every opening day” means Tuesday to Sunday during opening hours.
- All items are made or stocked in small batches. This document does not confirm whether an item is currently in stock.
- Staff must confirm same-day availability, temporary closures, and any price changes.
- Listed prices are the fictional final menu prices. There is no separate service charge or takeaway packaging fee in this content version.
- There are no published discounts, delivery fees, loyalty rewards, or promotional bundles.
- See `03-customisation.md` for additions and `02-ingredients-and-allergens.md` for recipe details.


## Printed menu reference (provided by the project team)

The printed Filmatch menu confirms all ten items, prices, drink sizes and descriptions above. Its featured Perfect Pairs are Classic Matcha Latte + Puto Duo (PHP 210), Pure Matcha + Leche Flan (PHP 215), and Coconut Cloud Matcha + Kutsinta Trio (PHP 230). The printed Make It Yours section lists oat +PHP 25, soy +PHP 20, and coconut +PHP 20 for Classic, Ube, and Strawberry; strong matcha (3 g) +PHP 30; adjustable sweetness and less/no ice free where supported by the detailed customisation guide. Its hours are Tuesday–Friday 11 AM–8 PM, Saturday–Sunday 10 AM–9 PM, Monday closed, last orders 30 minutes before closing. It warns of milk, egg, soy, wheat and coconut across items and shared equipment. This is a summary, not a change to the detailed item-specific rules. The cafe is fictional.
