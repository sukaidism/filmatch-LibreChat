# Filmatch

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Café visitors asking about matcha drinks, Filipino desserts, ingredients, customisations,
pairings, opening hours, and policies in a fictional student demonstration.

## Product Purpose

Ask Filmatch helps visitors understand the café's documented offering through conversation.
Success means clear, useful answers grounded in the existing café knowledge.

## Operating Context

An existing locally running LibreChat application with authentication, saved conversations,
and Gemini connectivity. Preserve these workflows and the existing model configuration.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Explain the documented menu, ingredients, customisations, pairings, hours, and policies.
- Preserve message-by-message English/Filipino matching and suppression of source filenames.
- Preserve the fictional café boundary: no orders, payments, reservations, live inventory,
  or allergy-safety guarantees. Do not fabricate missing café facts.
- Keep credentials private. Retain LibreChat attribution in credits or About.

## Brand Commitments

The product is Filmatch; the assistant is Ask Filmatch. Use a simple text wordmark until
the final logo is available. The confirmed welcome is “Matcha, merienda, and a moment to
slow down.” The interface is restrained and contemporary, with warm cream, deep matcha
green, charcoal text, generous spacing, and clear typography in light and dark modes.

## Evidence on Hand

`../librechat.filmatch.yaml` contains the configured assistant, café knowledge snapshot,
language policy, and theme. The café and customer scenarios are fictional. Final logo
artwork has not been supplied.

## Product Principles

- Make asking a café question the clearest action.
- Keep information readable and controls discoverable on desktop and mobile.
- Preserve working conversations and account workflows while refining their presentation.
- Distinguish documented information from unavailable or live information.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve keyboard navigation, accessible labels, visible focus, and reduced motion.
Verify text contrast and responsive layouts in both light and dark modes.
