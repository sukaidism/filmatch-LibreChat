# Filmatch chat and authentication

Mode: Operate. The user has pinned the visual direction and the existing working app.
Build path: code-first for this app screen; no standing marketing workflow preference recorded.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A café conversation starts with an easy question. Keep the working chat interface,
and give café information priority over advanced model controls.

OWN-WORLD: Warm cream canvas, charcoal text, deep matcha actions, and subtly tinted sidebar
and message surfaces. Dark mode uses deep green neutrals and pale matcha accents. Use the
existing self-hosted Inter face, a simple Filmatch wordmark, and semantic component tokens.

STORY: Visitors recognise Filmatch, read its short welcome, and ask about the menu,
customisations, pairings, or hours. Acknowledged fictional context and LibreChat credits
remain discoverable. Their chat history, account, and language behavior continue to work.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A quiet header, history sidebar, centred welcome at 24–30px within 28rem,
four concise questions in a two-column group, and the existing composer beneath it.
Mobile keeps the drawer and accessible overflow controls, without a competing header wordmark.
Login repeats the identity and offers the existing authentication form.

FORM: Existing LibreChat application structure, explicitly retained by the user. No
concept roll: the brief fixes the café direction. Signature interaction is a suggested
question entering the existing conversation flow. Keep motion brief and honour reduced motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review,
the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
